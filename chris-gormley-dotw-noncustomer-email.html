Would you still be following up on day 145?

Sometimes a missed call becomes a missed appointment, then a delay, then silence. That’s what nearly happened to Chris Gormley, with $30,160 on the line.

Chris put the inherited property under agreement with no rehab planned and enough room for a novation. Then the seller became difficult to reach and stopped consistently providing access.

The appraisal was rescheduled. More inspection work, a plumbing issue, a $750 credit, moving expenses, lender conditions, and shifting closing details followed.

There wasn’t one dramatic save. Chris’s team kept tracking and solving the next problem.

On Day 145, the deal closed with a reported $30,160 profit.

“Make sure you’re extra clear in the beginning, once a contract is signed, that calls need to be answered and sellers need to be responsive.”

— Chris Gormley
