Deal of the Week · Killeen, TX

Rickey offered $15K less than the other guy but still got the deal. Here’s how!

Imagine walking into a four-bedroom house in a good neighborhood. Built in 2003. Well maintained. Almost nothing to repair.

Most investors would wonder where the discount could possibly come from.

Rickey Miller saw an opportunity. The seller was relocating, and the property looked like a good candidate for a quick wholetail.

There was just one problem: other investors were offering roughly $15,000 more.

The four-bedroom, three-bathroom Killeen property.

So why did the seller choose Rickey?

The lead came from a Facebook ad. Rickey, who was local, called that same day and visited the property the following day.

The numbers made sense for a light-cleanup resale. Rickey discussed his offer and followed up in writing.

Then the competing offers entered the picture.

Instead of trying to outbid everyone, he continued building a relationship with the seller. He listened to what the seller wanted from the transaction and stayed in communication as the decision took shape.

He secured the $225,000 purchase and pursued a wholetail strategy with less than $2,000 in renovation work. The outcome: $60,279 in reported profit.

“Call every lead, have the conversation, and if a deal isn’t there, focus on helping the homeowner find the right path forward.”

— Rickey Miller
